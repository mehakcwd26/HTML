About Deepfake Detection

What is Deepfake?

Deepfake is AI-generated or manipulated image, audio or video content that can make someone appear to say or do things they did not.

How It Works

An AI model analyzes the uploaded media and identifies patterns that may indicate manipulation.

Why Detection?

It helps people evaluate digital content and improves awareness about manipulated media and misinformation.
