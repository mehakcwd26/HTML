About Deepfake Detection

Learn about deepfakes, artificial intelligence and the importance of digital media verification.

🧠

What is a Deepfake?

A deepfake is digitally manipulated or AI-generated media that can make a person appear to say or do something they never actually did.

🔍

What is Deepfake Detection?

Deepfake detection uses artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to examine media and identify possible signs of manipulation.

🛡️

Why is it Important?

It helps raise awareness about misinformation, identity misuse, manipulated content and the importance of digital authenticity.
